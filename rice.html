FAQ

Common
Questions

What makes wild rice different from regular rice?

Wild rice isn't technically rice at all — it's the seed of an aquatic grass. It has a nuttier, earthier flavour and a firmer bite than polished white or brown rice.

Is wild rice gluten-free?

Yes, wild rice is naturally gluten-free, and it's a good source of fibre and plant-based protein.

How do I cook wild rice?

Rinse the rice, then simmer in about 3 parts water to 1 part rice for 40-50 minutes, until the grains split and turn tender. Use it as a base for bowls, salads, or alongside roasted vegetables.

How is it packaged and shipped?

Wild rice comes in resealable 200g and 1kg bags, shipped from Riga with fast delivery across Latvia and the Baltics.
